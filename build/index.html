No But, Really, Seriously, what is this for anyway?

We like trading files for various reasons and sometimes the easiest way to do that is with a web page. But maybe you want to limit access to that page. Every sharing service we have run in to makes you create an account (or turn over other credentials) and turn over a credit card and charges recurring fees and then bombards you with emails about features you don’t care about for months.

This is a one-time service. You pay us, we encrypt your files and give you the encryption key in the form of a QR code. It comes with some risks. If you lose your key, that’s it. We can’t get to your files. Our system is designed such that we can’t get to your files, which also means we don’t know whose files belong to whom. That is the idea. It’s a web locker. Forget the combination or lose the key… whoops.

But also… you don’t need to know who is getting the files if they have a key. You could give out ten keys to the same locker for a club or a class. You could have a store giveaway that takes place only at the store. You could distirbute music or book files that you don’t want freely available.

We think there’s a ton of uses for QR key lockers and we are working on new ones, too!—like a safety deposit style loqr that requires two simultaneous keys!
